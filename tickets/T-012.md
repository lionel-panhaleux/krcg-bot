## Problem

On 2026-07-26 an `eng` session tried to source Discord's "≤5 action rows per message" limit, failed to find it, and wrote that into `PLAN.md`'s Queue as an upstream-docs fact — that `MAX_ACTION_ROWS = 5` and the matching line in `.claude/rules/bot-code.md` might be "folklore". The false claim sat in the plan of record for two days.

It was re-sourced on 2026-07-28 in minutes. The failure was entirely tooling, in three layers: `discord.com/developers/docs/*` now 301-redirects **cross-host** to `docs.discord.com/developers/*`; the relevant line sits at 2539 of a 2561-line page, so a fetch truncates before reaching it; and — reproduced independently by the reviewer of this session's own commit — `WebFetch`'s summarizer *silently drops the sentence* even when the page is retrieved whole. Only reading the raw page surfaces it. The session read a tooling failure as an absence of documentation.

The third layer is the dangerous one: a summarized fetch looks like a successful fetch. Nothing in the result announces that the answer was dropped on the way.

CLAUDE.md already said "Tech claims: current docs first (context7), never training-data recall." That governs *where* a claim comes from. It says nothing about what a fetch that comes back empty is allowed to prove.

## Outcome

No doc in the repo asserts that something is undocumented on the strength of a failed fetch.
